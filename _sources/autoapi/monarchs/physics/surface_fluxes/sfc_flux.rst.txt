monarchs.physics.surface_fluxes.sfc_flux
========================================

.. py:function:: monarchs.physics.surface_fluxes.sfc_flux(cell, met_data, xsurf)

   Calculate the surface heat flux from the input shortwave and longwave
   fluxes and latent/sensible heat fluxes.

   :Parameters: * **cell** (*numpy structured array*) -- Element of the model grid.
                * **met_data** (*numpy structured array*) -- Meteorological data for the current timestep (``LW_down``, ``SW_down``,
                  ``temperature``, ``surf_pressure``, ``dew_point_temperature``, ``wind``).
                * **xsurf** (*float*) -- Surface temperature. Taken from our initial guess x (i.e. x[0]) [K].

   :returns: **Q** -- Surface energy flux. [W m^-2].
   :rtype: float64
