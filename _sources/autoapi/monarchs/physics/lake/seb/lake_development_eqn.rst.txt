monarchs.physics.lake.seb.lake_development_eqn
==============================================

.. py:function:: monarchs.physics.lake.seb.lake_development_eqn(x, args)

   Residual function used to solve the surface energy balance when
   we have a lake on top of the firn.

   Solved by lake_seb_solver with formation=True.

   :Parameters: * **x** (*float*) -- Current estimate of the lake surface temperature [K].
                * **args** (*tuple*) -- ``(cell, met_data, T_core)``, where T_core is the mid-column lake
                  temperature [K].
