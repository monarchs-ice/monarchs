monarchs.physics.lake.seb.lake_formation_eqn
============================================

.. py:function:: monarchs.physics.lake.seb.lake_formation_eqn(x, args)

   Residual function used to solve the surface energy balance when
   we have a lake forming on the surface.

   Solved by lake_seb_solver with formation=False.

   :Parameters: * **x** (*float*) -- Current estimate of the lake surface temperature [K].
                * **args** (*tuple*) -- ``(cell, met_data, k, T1, dz_firn)``, where k is the firn thermal
                  conductivity, T1 the second-layer firn temperature, and dz_firn the
                  firn layer thickness.
